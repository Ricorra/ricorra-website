How it Works

Simple by design.

Three steps to your first recurring Bitcoin subscription.

1
Create your plan

Set a name, price in USD, and billing interval. Ricorra converts to BTC at payment time using the live exchange rate.

2
Share your link

Send subscribers your unique payment page. They pay their first invoice directly in Bitcoin — no account required.

3
Get paid directly

Payments arrive in your wallet — not ours. Ricorra is non-custodial. We never hold, touch, or move your Bitcoin.
